Library

Supported: GIF, WebP, APNG, PNG, WebM, MP4, and Lottie (.json) animations; MP3, WAV, OGG, and M4A sounds. Videos (WebM/MP4) play their own sound; the sound chosen above only plays if the video is silent. Files you drop into the library folder show up here the next time you open this window.
